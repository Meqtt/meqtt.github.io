Subscriptions and Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. You can manage or cancel subscriptions from your device’s Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions page.

Restore Purchases

Open InstaText and use the Restore Purchases option on the subscription screen. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the original purchase.
